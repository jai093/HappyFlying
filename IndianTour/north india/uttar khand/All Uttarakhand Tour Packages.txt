Best Places to Visit Uttarakhand
If you’re planning Uttarakhand trip packages, just know this state is brimming with natural beauty, adventure, and culture. Whether it’s the serene hill stations or the sacred rivers, Uttarakhand tourist places are perfect for anyone looking to soak in nature and history. Here’s a rundown of the ideal places to visit in Uttarakhand to make your Uttarakhand tour truly memorable:

Nainital (The Lake District):
Nainital is perhaps one of the most visited tourist destinations in Uttarakhand, primarily because of the eye-shaped Naini Lake. The town lies amidst hills and offers boat rides, scenic points like Snow View and Tiffin Top, and bustling markets. The best time for a trip to Nainital with Uttarakhand packages is between March and June, as the weather is perfect for a visit.

Rishikesh and Haridwar:
Among the attractions in Uttarakhand packages, the twin towns of Rishikesh and Haridwar have no competitors. Rishikesh is perfect for adrenaline junkies as well as those seeking peace. You can try river rafting on the Ganges or meditate by the riverbank. Meanwhile, Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri in Haridwar is a divine experience that simply should not be missed. The best time to visit Uttarakhand for such towns is during the cooler months, from October to March.

Mussoorie (The Queen of Hills):
Mussoorie is one of the best places in Uttarakhand for a getaway. Its misty mornings and colonial charm are reasons why it tops the list in Uttarakhand tour packages. Some of the must-visit spots include Kempty Falls, Camel’s Back Road, and Gun Hill. Strolls amidst nature are a treat in Mussoorie’s lush trails.

Auli:
For those looking for unique places to see in Uttarakhand, Auli is a must. The main attraction here is the crystal-clear slopes and the excellent panoramic views of the Himalayas. It is also one of the prime centres for skiing. If you’re not a skiing lover, the cable car from Joshimath to Auli offers breathtaking views that you can’t find anywhere else. This might be one of the ideal places to visit in Uttarakhand during winter.

Ranikhet and Almora (Offbeat):
Ranikhet and Almora have serene retreats and also stand as one of the best places for nature lovers. These hill stations are full of pine forests and old temples. These places are meant for those who want some relief from the bustling and crowded tourist destinations in Uttarakhand packages.

Jim Corbett National Park:
If wildlife is on your mind, then Jim Corbett is one of the most captivating Uttarakhand places to visit. Renowned for its tiger population, the park also hosts safaris to spot other wildlife like elephants and deer. An overnight stay at a forest lodge adds a touch of adventure to your Uttarakhand trip packages.

Beaches by the Riverbanks:
While Uttarakhand packages are more famous for its hills, Rishikesh’s riverbanks, like the beaches of Ram Jhula and Shivpuri, are ideal for unwinding. You can relax on the sandy shores of the Ganges, which offers a beach-like experience in the mountains.

Munsiyari:
Nestled in the Pithoragarh district is Munsiyari, heaven for trekkers and nature enthusiasts. Besides the stunning Panchachuli peaks, Munsiyari is a picture-perfect town with eye-stopping scenery, unblemished beauty, and challenging trekking routes like Khalia Top and Milam Glacier. Plan a Uttarakhand trip without forgetting this pearl of a town.

Peora:
If you wish to cut yourself off from the world, Peora is where you should go. This off-the-beaten-path Kumaoni village is famous for its beauty, fruit orchards, and eco-tourism atmosphere. It’s a perfect choice for a peaceful retreat and must feature in your Uttarakhand packages.

Kausani:
If you’re looking to have a serene Uttarakhand experience, you need to be in Kausani. With panoramic Himalayan views, this little-known gem is all about nature. From having tea at Kausani Tea Estate, visiting Baijnath Temple, or having a serene retreat, this place has it all.

Khirsu:
Nested between oak and deodar woodlands, Khirsu is one of the quietest destinations for a Uttarakhand vacation. With breathtaking Himalayan scenery, trekking, and the picturesque Ghandiyal Devi Temple, the destination is a heaven for solitude lovers.

Binsar:
Binsar is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts and birdwatchers. Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary, Zero Point, and old temples are reasons enough to include them in your Uttarakhand tour packages. The tranquillity and splendour here are simply unbeatable!

Chakrata:
For an entirely offbeat experience, Chakrata is a total treat. It is situated at an altitude of 7,000 feet and provides adventure sports such as trekking, skiing, and caving. While booking Uttarakhand tour packages, include Chakrata for a wonderful experience.

Cool Facts: The holy Ganga, India’s most revered river, originates from the Gangotri Glacier in Uttarakhand. It is a place of deep spiritual significance, attracting millions of pilgrims every year.

The best time to visit Uttarakhand
If you’re planning a Uttarakhand trip package, timing is everything to make the most of your visit. The best time to visit Uttarakhand depends on what you’re looking for in your Uttarakhand tour packages. Let’s break it down season by season.

Summer season in Uttarakhand from March to June:
For most travellers, summer remains the peak season and undoubtedly the ideal time to visit Uttarakhand. The weather is pleasant throughout the hill stations like Nainital, Mussoorie, and Auli. Daytime temperatures oscillate between 20 degrees and 35 degrees which is ideal for sight-seeing and exploration of so many places to visit in Uttarakhand. It is during this period when the demand for Uttarakhand tour packages is extremely high; hence it is advised to book in advance.

Monsoon season in Uttarakhand from July to September:
Monsoons are pretty tricky times to travel in as they can lead to heavy rain showers and cause landslides, thus disrupting the plans. It is an off-peak season, though, so if you enjoy the greenery and don’t mind a bit of rain, it could be a great time for deals on Uttarakhand travel packages. But be cautious as roads can be slippery.

Winter season in Uttarakhand from October to February: 
Winter is apt for those who love the snow and chilly weather. The ideal time to visit Uttarakhand for snowfall is between December and February, especially in Auli and Chopta. It is a less crowded time, perfect for someone looking for a serene experience with Uttarakhand trip packages. The temperature dips to 0°C or below; so you will need to carry proper warm clothing. Choose your Uttarakhand tour packages based on which season suits your plans, and get ready for an amazing experience in Uttarakhand.

How to reach Uttarakhand?
Planning your Uttarakhand trip packages starts with figuring out how to get there. Let me walk you through the best ways to reach this beautiful state and ensure your Uttarakhand itinerary goes smoothly.

By Air:
If you prefer flying, then Jolly Grant Airport is available in Dehradun city. It links up all the main Indian cities including Delhi and Mumbai. After you have touched down, you can easily find taxis or book transport as part of your Uttarakhand tour packages to reach various places to visit in Uttarakhand.
By Train:
Travelling by train is both convenient and budget-friendly. The three major railway stations connecting Uttarakhand to the other cities of the country are Haridwar, Dehradun, and Kathgodam. Based on your itinerary, you can travel by train to any of these stations and then reach your desired Uttarakhand tourist attraction by road.
By Road:
You can reach Uttarakhand by road with a flexible and scenic travel experience. The state is well connected through national highways and state roads. If you want to drive your vehicle or take the bus, it is the best mode for you when you want to travel at your own pace with Uttarakhand holiday packages. Whichever way you choose, your Uttarakhand travel is bound to be unforgettable with the right Uttarakhand tour packages!
What to eat?
When planning your Uttarakhand trip packages, make sure to explore the local flavours that make Uttarakhand food so unique. Here’s a list of must-try dishes and where to find them on your Uttarakhand travel packages.

Aloo Ke Gutke:
This spicy potato preparation is one of the must-try dishes in any Uttarakhand eatery, and its flavour is complete when enjoyed in any roadside dhabas of Almora. Perfect for a quick snack when you are on your trip with Uttarakhand travel packages.

Bhatt Ki Churkani:
This black soybean curry is high in protein content and can be relished in each corner of the state. You can enjoy it at local eating places in Dehradun to complement your Uttarakhand travel guide.

Kafuli:
For those looking for something healthy, Kafuli—a spinach-based curry—is a must-try. You’ll find it served at authentic restaurants in Nainital and Ranikhet. Adding this to your itinerary will be a treat for your taste buds.

Bal Mithai:
You haven’t savoured the true essence of Uttarakhand cuisine, without indulging the fudge-like sweet in sugar balls, Bal Mithai. This has originated in Almora and should feature in the list of travel to Uttarakhand. Add these culinary gems to your Uttarakhand holiday packages for a complete experience!

Restaurants to Visit
When diving into Uttarakhand food during your Uttarakhand travel packages, make sure you visit these top restaurants for an authentic taste:

Chotiwala Restaurant (Rishikesh):
Chotiwala is another name that goes hand in hand with the tourism of Uttarakhand. The restaurant dishes out mouth-watering vegetarian dishes that truly and aptly represent the essence of Uttarakhand cuisine. Ideal for those visiting Uttarakhand to seek spiritual enlightenment yet a foodie at heart.
Sakley’s Restaurant & Pastry Shop (Nainital):
For continental food and desserts, Sakley’s is a great choice. It is an adorable shop located in the heart of Nainital. It’s perfect after a day spent at the top places to visit in Uttarakhand.
Kalsang Friends Corner (Dehradun and Mussoorie):
Known for its Tibetan and Chinese offerings, this spot is ideal for adding a bit of variety to your Uttarakhand trip plan. The momos and thukpa here are a must-try.
The Birdcage (Mukteshwar):
For an interesting view with a cup of coffee, The Birdcage offers you a fusion of local as well as global cuisine. This would be a wonderful addition to some lazy, laid-back Uttarakhand travel packages experience.
Cool Facts: Behind the famous Badrinath temple, there is a hidden cave called the ‘Vyas Gufa,’ where it is believed that Ved Vyasa meditated and composed the Mahabharata. The cave is tucked away from the usual tourist routes, making it an intriguing spot for history buffs.

Honeymoon Places in Uttarakhand
If you’re planning a romantic Uttarakhand tour, this state is perfect for couples looking to create cherished memories. Let me guide you through the ideal places in Uttarakhand for your honeymoon.

Lansdowne:
This is a destination that is the best for couples in Uttarakhand for a quiet holiday. Pine forests and old colonial buildings surround the town of Lansdowne, where slow walks and views of scenic beauty are available. It is a place which often gets overlooked in the tourist map of Uttarakhand.

Bhimtal:
If you want a quieter version of Nainital, you will look no further than Bhimtal. With its picture-perfect lake and quaint surroundings, it happens to be one of the best Uttarakhand honeymoon destinations to visit for an intimate experience.

Chopta:
Chopta is known as the ‘Mini Switzerland of India’ and is a perfect destination for nature and adventure-loving couples. The trek to Tungnath and Chandrashila offers breathtaking panoramic views, making it one of the top Uttarakhand tourist attractions.

Kausani:
For those who love scenic views, Kausani is the destination that offers panoramic views of the Himalayas. It is a top destination to visit in Uttarakhand because of its sunsets and serene ambience. Providing tea gardens and the Rudradhari Falls, it is a perfect destination for quiet walks and cosy evenings. Add Kausani to your Uttarakhand trip packages to make your honeymoon truly special.

Kanatal:
Kanatal happens to be an ideal place for couples, who want to spend quiet and scenic time. Apple orchards and pine forests surround this beautiful village which is one of the best places to visit in Uttarakhand for romantic getaways. Camping and nature trails of Kanatal add a tinge of adventure to your Uttarakhand tour.

Shopping in Uttarakhand
When planning your Uttarakhand trip packages, don’t forget to include some time for shopping. Uttarakhand shopping is a delightful experience, and there are plenty of places where you can pick up unique items. Here are some top places to visit for shopping:

Nainital’s Mall Road:
Perfect for candle shopping, woollen garments, and woodwork, no Uttarakhand tour itinerary can be considered complete without visiting Nainital’s Mall Road. The beautiful candle shops selling candles in the shapes of animals and various fragrances can be great souvenirs.

Bhotia Market:
Bhotia Market has genuine Pashmina shawls, woollens, and handwoven carpets. It is a vibrant place where you can buy some of the best local artistry.

Paltan Bazaar (Dehradun):
For any Uttarakhand travel packages, Paltan Bazaar is the right place for affordable items, spices, handicrafts, and even Garhwali traditional jewellery.

Lala Bazaar (Almora):
Lala Bazaar in Almora is famous for its copperware and Angora wool products. Therefore, it is a must-visit for Uttarakhand tour packages.

Did you know that the famous “Valley of Flowers” wasn’t always on the map?

The Valley of Flowers, a UNESCO World Heritage site, remained hidden from the outside world until the early 1930s when it was discovered by British mountaineers. It’s only in recent decades that it has gained fame for its breathtaking meadows, making it an offbeat paradise for nature lovers.

Things to do in Uttarakhand
When planning your itinerary, make sure to pack it with experiences that showcase the region’s beauty and culture. Here are the top things to do in Uttarakhand to make your trip unforgettable.

Trekking in the Himalayas:
Hitting the trails is one of the adventure activities that Uttarakhand boasts of. Amongst the other popular trails among top Uttarakhand tourist spots, the Valley of Flowers and Roopkund Trek are touted for their breathtaking panoramic views.
Jim Corbett National Park Wildlife Spotting:
Spotting wildlife in Jim Corbett National Park should be on your list of places in Uttarakhand. It’s one of the most exhilarating sites to see in nature.
Whitewater Rafting in Rishikesh:
If you are an adventure-seeker, then you are bound to visit the Ganges for river rafting in Rishikesh. It is also popular as the adventure capital of India. Rishikesh can be called the haven when it comes to offering such rapids, thus bestowing it as the major place in Uttarakhand travel packages for water sports.
Visiting Temples and Ashrams:
Exploring sacred sites like Kedarnath and Badrinath adds a spiritual touch to your journey and ranks among the ideal places to visit in Uttarakhand.
Tasting Local food:
Sampling foods in Uttarakhand is a must. From Aloo Ke Gutke to Bal Mithai, indulging in local delicacies is a wonderful way to experience Uttarakhand tourism at its finest.
