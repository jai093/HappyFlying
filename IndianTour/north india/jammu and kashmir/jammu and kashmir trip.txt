Best Places to Visit Jammu and Kashmir
 

Whenever you think of Jammu and Kashmir, you probably imagine snow-capped mountains, green valleys, and serene lakes. You are right! A Jammu and Kashmir trip consists of plunging into the beauty of nature and further acquainting yourself with the cultural richness of this paradise. While going for a Jammu and Kashmir tour, these are some best places to visit in kashmir :

Famous Places to Visit
Gulmarg: For those fond of adventure and craving skiing or a cable car ride, known as the Gulmarg Gondola, this should be included in your bucket list while travelling to Kashmir. Snow here in winter is quite fascinating; even in summer, it’s cool as an escape destination.
Srinagar: Known for its iconic Dal Lake and beautifully crafted houseboats, Srinagar is a prime destination in any Kashmir trip package. You’ll love the Mughal Gardens, especially Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh, which are perfect for a stroll.
Pahalgam: Ideal for nature lovers, Pahalgam offers scenic views of rivers and valleys. You can also embark on treks here, making it a key stop in most Kashmir travel packages.
Sonmarg: Famous for its meadow of gold, Sonmarg offers jaw-dropping views of glaciers and mountains. For trekking and camp lovers, it stands out as the first choice and thus finds its place in most of the Kashmir tour itineraries.
Jammu: It is the city of temples, with the most famous one being the Vaishno Devi shrine. If you are religiously inclined or enjoy history, the inclusion of a visit here in your Jammu Kashmir package is a must.
Hill Stations to Visit
Speaking of hill stations, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonmarg are considered to be the major ones. Other than that, cooler climatic conditions, great scenery, and respite from daily tussles are a few other things that these places can provide you. If you are fond of trekking or winter games, then these hill stations will make your Kashmir holiday package unforgettable.

The best time to visit Jammu and Kashmir
When it comes to planning a Jammu and Kashmir trip, timing can make all the difference. Depending on what kind of experience you’re after, the seasons in this region can offer you different views and adventures. Let me guide you through the best time to visit Kashmir, so you can make the most of your Jammu Kashmir package.

Spring (March to Early May)
If you are one of those people who love flowers in full blossom, then spring is the season for your Kashmir tour packages. Yes, the famous tulip gardens in Srinagar are in full bloom and cover a colourful landscape that would be ideal for shooting photographs and strolling quietly and peacefully. The temperature during this time is quite pleasant and ranges between 15°C to 18°C, hence, perfect for outdoors. This is a nice time to select packages for travel to Kashmir because if you like to enjoy good climatic conditions without the summer rush.

Summer (May to August)
Summer Kashmir trip packages will be a beautiful sight of green valleys, blue skies, and cool breezes. Temperature ranges between 20° to 30° C. So, quite pleasant for sightseeing tours around Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam. This is the peak tourist season; hence, if someone plans to opt for Kashmir holiday packages during summer, he or she needs to book well in advance as accommodations do not remain available during this period.

Autumn (September to November)
This is, certainly, one of the most beautiful seasons to plan a tour to Kashmir. With Chinars wearing golden colour, the mercury in the barometer finally becomes pleasant, ranging between 10°C and 20°C. Because there are fewer tourists during this time, autumn is considered to be among the best times for your Jammu and Kashmir tour package.

Winter (December to February)
For snow lovers, this is the time when real magic begins. This is the time for skiing in Gulmarg and for beholding the scenic beauty of snow-clad landscapes. The temperature during this time remains considerably low, with a range of -2°C to 12°C. For all those who love winter sports or wish to behold the breathtaking beauty of Kashmir blanketed under heavy snow, the Kashmir packages during these winter months would be ideal.

Peak vs Off-Season
The peak tourist season falls between May and August, and this is when a maximum number of visitors book Kashmir trips. Visiting off-season, like during autumn or winter, provides various Kashmir tour packages at a cheaper rate, which may be ideal for budget travellers. Whether it is a relaxed summer retreat or an adventure-packed snowy affair, a Jammu Kashmir package is available for all seasons!

How to reach Jammu and Kashmir?
When planning your Jammu and Kashmir trip, knowing how to get there is key to making your Kashmir itinerary smooth and hassle-free. Let’s go over the best options to help you choose what suits your Kashmir trip plan best.

The fastest and most comfortable way to reach Jammu Kashmir would be by air. The major airport is Srinagar International Airport, linking to the key metropolises of Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru. Once you reach the airport, a Kashmir tour itinerary can be arranged easily, including easy access to places such as Gulmarg and Pahalgam. This again is quite hassle-free since many Kashmir tour packages include an airport pickup service.

By Train
The nearby railway station is Jammu Tawi if you enjoy travelling by train. You can have easy access to most of the scenic places in Kashmir by road from here. There are regular trains from cities like Delhi, Kolkata, and Mumbai. Once you arrive in Jammu, your Jammu Kashmir package can include a scenic road trip to Srinagar, adding a unique touch to your Kashmir trip.

By Road
For the more adventurous, travelling by road offers breathtaking views. The National Highway 44 connects Jammu to Srinagar, making it ideal for road trips. Private cars and buses are readily available for those on Kashmir travel packages. This option allows you to take in the stunning landscapes at your own pace. Whichever way you reach, rest assured that your Kashmir holiday packages will be nothing but unforgettable!

What to eat Jammu and Kashmir?
When you’re on a Jammu and Kashmir trip, tasting the local cuisine is an absolute must! Let me guide you through the best places in Kashmir to savour some traditional Kashmir food.

Wazwan: A royal banquet of more than 30 dishes! You will have this traditional system of dining at Ahdoos in Srinagar. Your Kashmir tour itinerary would not be complete if you do not taste Wazwan.
Rogan Josh: The famous lamb curry, rich with aromatic spices, has to be tasted. You’ll get the best of it in Kashmir restaurants in Srinagar, particularly at Mughal Darbar.
Yakhni: A mild curd-based curry which is usually made with mutton. If your Kashmir trip plan involves visiting Pahalgam, then you should not miss trying Yakhni at Dana Pani.
Gushtaba: Mouth-watering meatball preparation, often served under the course of Wazwan. You will find it in most Kashmir restaurants such as Shamyana, and Srinagar.
Kahwa: This traditional Kashmiri tea, infused with saffron, almonds, and spices, is best enjoyed at Chai Jaai in Srinagar.
