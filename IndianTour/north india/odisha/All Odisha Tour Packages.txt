Chilika 
The Chilika lake is the largest brackish water lagoon in all of Asia and a massive tourist attraction in Odisha. It spreads over a massive area across the district of Ganjam, Khorda and Puri. It is a popular honeymoon destination for couples, with key attractions being the Honeymoon island, Nalabana island and Bird sanctuary, Rambha Bay, a romantic boat drive and lots more. It also features some fantastic resorts and local Odisha cuisine. Chilika is also one of the ideal Odisha places to visit in general. 

Gopalpur
Besides being one of the most beautiful places in Odisha, Gopalpur is also an excellent choice for a honeymoon destination. Its impressive beach and accommodations add to a serene holiday experience with your loved one. The Gopalpur lighthouse and the availability of water sports are some of the major attractions of this place. Puri

Best Hill stations and Beaches in Odisha:
If you are visiting Odisha for your next trip, you will be in for a versatile experience. Amidst the famous places in Odisha lie some of the most iconic beaches and hill stations.

Hill stations in Odisha
Daringbadi– Daringbadi, a small town with lush greenery and spellbinding hills, is popularly known as the “Kashmir of Odisha”. It is one of the best places to visit in Odisha if you want to capture the charming views of nature and its landscapes. You can also partake in the exciting activities Daringbadi has to offer, which include birdwatching, trekking and jungle safari.
Mahendragiri hills– Located about 40 kilometres from Paralakhemundi, Mahendragiri is yet another popular hill station in Odisha. It is the second-highest mountain peak in the state and is famous for its mesmerising natural beauty. The Mahendratanaya river flowing down in this location is a key attraction of this hill station in Orissa.  
Other hill stations–  You can also check out other hill stations in Odisha, like Dumuriput, Tensa Hill, Taptapani, Deuli Hill, Devagiri Range, Malayagiri Hills, and so on.

What to Eat in Odisha?
While you tour Odisha and experience its grand aspects, consider diving deep into the excellent local cuisine that the region has to offer. Here are some must-try Odia delicacies:

Khichdi
Macha ghanta
Rasabali
Chakuli pitha
Kanika
Khichdi
This special khichdi-variant is an immensely important dish in the land and culture of Odisha. This finger-licking nutritious delight is prepared with ghee, rice, light spices, lentil and chopped vegetables. Besides being a popular household meal, Khicede is also served as the primary “bhog” of the Shree Jagannath Temple in Puri.  

Macha ghanta
Having a magnificent coastline makes Odisha a place with tasty seafood dishes and recipes. Macha Ghanta is a prime example of such a dish, which is a fish curry prepared with chilli powder, chana dal, garlic, onion, tomatoes, cinnamon sticks and potatoes. Its delectable taste extends when paired with a salad and steamed rice.

Rasabali
This is a major Odia dessert, served as a part of Shree Jagannath Temple’s Chappan Bhog. It includes small vada-shaped tikkis made with wheat flour, paneer, sugar, and cardamom powder, which are deep-fried and soaked in flavoured milk.

Chakuli pitha
Pitha is a popular dish in India’s eastern region. The Chakuli pitha is one of its special variants made in Odisha with ingredients like urad dal and rice batter. The final product looks similar to tortillas and is served with curry, ghugni, pickles or aloo bhaja.

Kanika
Kanika is a local favourite, often prepared during auspicious or special occasions. It is also a part of Shree Jagannath Temple’s Chappan Bhog and is prepared with basmati rice, cashew nuts, ghee, cinnamon, sugar and cardamom as the main ingredients. This sweet dish can be enjoyed as a dessert or a main course alongside veg and non-veg curry. 

Shopping in Odisha?
Odisha has some of the greatest local craftworks and markets for ideal souvenir shopping. If you are wondering what to shop in Odisha, here are some options you can consider:

Jewellery work
Wooden mask
Konark wheel
Sarees
Jewellery work
Odisha is home to some of the finest works of jewellery craftsmanship in the country. These handmade pieces, which include jewellery boxes, earrings, necklaces and so on, can be an excellent gifts for yourself or a loved one. Look for Filigree work for the finest silver carved ornaments available in the major cities of the state.  

Wooden mask
The hand-painted wooden masks in Odisha are great examples of the immaculate skills of the local artisans. These souvenirs help pack the essence of the region’s tradition with you to your home. These wooden masks of Hindu deities can be ideal home decor items, and the best place to buy them is at Raghurajpur. It is also one of the best places to see in Orissa. 

Konark wheel
The Konark wheel is a beautiful and must-have souvenir you can buy during your visit to the Sun Temple. This souvenir resembles the wheels of the Sun Temple in a miniature format, usually carved in stone or wood. 

Sarees
The handlooms in Odisha produce some of the most beautiful sarees in the eastern region of India. This is why shopping for many tourists largely includes checking out and buying famous sarees from well-known places. For saree shopping, you can check out Pasapalli sarees (Bargarh district), Bomkai sarees (Ganjam district), Kotpad sarees (Koraput), and so on.

If you are shopping in Bhubaneswar, you can check out Ekamra Haat, OSHC on Rajpath road, Pipli, BMC Keshari Mall, and so on. For shopping in Puri, you can visit Swargadwarda market, Chandan bazaar, Red Cross road, and other local markets.

Adventurous Things to Do in Odisha:

The Puri Konark Marine Drive, Tampara Lake and Chilika are some of the best places in Odisha where you can partake in this thrilling activity. You can also find scuba diving, snorkelling, paddle surfing, rafting and other water sports experiences at Ramachandi beach. 
Trekking– Camping and trekking go hand in hand, and you can avail of both across various nature camps in Odisha. For this adventurous activity, you can check out the nature camps at Simlipal, Satkosia, Debigarh and Bhitarkanika.
Camel/horseback riding– For some light-hearted thrilling rides through the sandy beach of Odisha, you can consider going for horse or camelback riding. You can take on this activity at any popular beach in Odisha, including Puri beach.

During this tour, you will be able to see animals like Gharial crocodiles, elephants, white tigers, a variety of birds, deer and much more. Popular Odisha wildlife tours and safari sites include Nandankanan, Simlipal, Ratnagiri, Satkosia, and so on.
