Day Wise Itinerary
1st Day : Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar, Maharastra, – Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar
Approx Distance: 250 Km • Est. Travel Time: 6 hours
Arrive at Mumbai , and proceed to Mahabaleshwar , enrotue visit Panchgini, on arrival check inn to pre booked hotel , overnight stay at Mahabaleshwar

2nd Day : Mahabaleshwar, Maharastra, – MAHABALESHWAR LOCAL
After breakfast, take a local tour covering sites of Mahabaleshwar. Mahabaleshwar Temple, Arthur Point, Echo Point, Strawberry farms, Lodwick Point & Wilson Point. After lunch rest of the day is free for shopping. Overnight Stay in Mahabaleshwar
( Note : All sightseeing is subject to availability )

3rd Day : Mahabaleshwar, Maharastra, to Lonavala, Maharastra – Mahabaleshwar to Lonavala Via Khandala
Approx Distance: 180 Km • Est. Travel Time: 4 hours
After Breakfast check out from hotel and proceed to visit some sightseeing in Khandala like Lion Point, Bhor Ghat, Valvan Dam, Rajmachi Garden, Karla Caves And the Monsoon Lake after that proceed hotel and overnight stay at Lonavala
( Note : All sightseeing is subject to availability )

4th Day: Lonavala, Maharastra to Lonavala, Maharastra – LONAVALA LOCAL
After breakfast proceed to visit local sightseeing like Kune Falls, Lonavala Lake, Sunil’s Celebrity Wax Museum, Tungarli Lake, Shree Narayani Dham Temple, Ryewood Park,Lion Point, Bhor Ghat, Valvan Dam , that proceed hotel and overnight stay at Lonavala
( Note : All sightseeing is subject to availability )

5th Day: Lonavala, Maharastra to Mumbai – Drop
After breakfast check out from the hotel and proceed to Mumbai drop at Airport / railway station for onward journey
( Note : Only transfer no sightseeing in Mumbai )
