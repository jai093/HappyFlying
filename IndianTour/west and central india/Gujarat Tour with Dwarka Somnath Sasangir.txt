Tour Highlights
Visit the legendary Somnath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.
Explore the ancient city of Dwarka, home to the famous Dwarkadhish Temple.
Discover the wildlife at Sasan Gir National Park, the only place in the world to see Asiatic lions in the wild.
