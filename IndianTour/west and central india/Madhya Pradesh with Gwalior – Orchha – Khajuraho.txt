Discover the rich heritage of Madhya Pradesh on this captivating 5-night package tour featuring 2 nights in Gwalior, 1 night in Orchha, and 2 nights in Khajuraho. This itinerary blends majestic forts, ancient temples, and UNESCO wonders, offering a seamless journey through central India’s historical heartland.​

Gwalior Highlights (2 Nights)
Start in Gwalior with visits to the iconic Gwalior Fort atop a sandstone hill, featuring the ornate Man Singh Palace and panoramic city views. Explore the lavish Jai Vilas Palace, home to one of Asia’s largest chandeliers and royal artifacts, plus local markets for cultural immersion.​

Orchha Experience (1 Night)
Travel 3-4 hours to Orchha on the Betwa River for a serene stay. Discover the grand Jahangir Mahal, mural-filled Raja Mahal, and spiritual Ram Raja Temple, where Lord Ram is revered as king, evoking 16th-17th century Bundela splendor.​

Khajuraho Wonders (2 Nights)
Conclude in Khajuraho, a UNESCO site with Chandela-era temples from the 10th-12th centuries. Tour the Western, Eastern, and Southern groups, renowned for intricate carvings symbolizing life’s facets, alongside local markets for souvenirs.

Trip Plan:
1st Day: Gwalior – Arrival and Sightseeing
Pick-up from Gwalior Station/Airport, Sightseeing at Gurudwara Data Bandi Chhod Sahib, Gwalior Fort. In the evening, enjoy the Light & Sound Show, Night Halt at Gwalior.

2nd Day: Gwalior – Sightseeing
After Breakfast, Sightseeing at Jai Vilas Palace, Birla Temple (Sun Temple), Tansen ka Makbara, Tomb of Muhammad Ghaus and Night Halt at Gwalior.

3rd Day: Gwalior to Orchha – Transfer via Jhansi
After Breakfast, transfer to Orchha via Jhansi Fort Sightseeing, later Orchha Sightseeing at Orchha Fort and Night Halt at Orchha.

4th Day: Orchha to Khajuraho – Transfer and Sightseeing
After Breakfast, Sightseeing at Orchha, Chaturbhuj Temple, Ram Raja Mandir & Minaxi Mandir, River Rafting at Betwa River Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal, Sheesh Mahal, and transfer to Khajuraho.

5th Day: Khajuraho – Sightseeing
After Breakfast, Sightseeing at the Famous Western & Eastern Group of Temples, Pandav Fall & Raneh Falls, Light & Sound Show and Night Halt at Khajuraho.

6th Day: Khajuraho to Gwalior – Departure
After Breakfast, depart for Gwalior Airport/Station.
