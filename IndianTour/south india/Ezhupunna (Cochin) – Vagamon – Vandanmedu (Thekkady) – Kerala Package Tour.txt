Ezhupunna (Cochin) Vagamon Vandanmedu (Thekkady) 4 Nights and 5 Days Package Tour
Premium Getaway
A tailor-made 4 nights and 5 days Kerala tour package covering Ezhupunna (near Cochin), the hill station of Vagamon, and the spice plantations of Vandanmedu (Thekkady) offers a serene blend of backwater proximity, misty hills, and wildlife adventures. 

Key Highlights of the Package:
Ezhupunna: A scenic village, is renowned for its tranquil backwaters, lush coconut grooves, and authentic, rustic Kerala village life.
Vagamon: Known for its pine forests, meadows, and cool climate.
Vandanmedu/Thekkady: Famous for being the heart of cardamom plantation tourism and wildlife.
Activities: Paragliding, Trekking, Boating in Periyar Lake, Jeep Safari, Spice Plantation Tours.
