Embark on a 5N/6D Mysore-Coorg-Ooty Hill Station Escape!
Unwind in the misty embrace of South India’s premier hill destinations with our curated Mysore 1N – Coorg 2N – Ooty 2N Package Tour. Start your journey in royal Mysore, marveling at the iconic Mysore Palace illuminated at dusk and savoring fragrant silk sarees at Devaraja Market. Venture into the lush coffee plantations of Coorg (Kodagu) for treks through evergreen estates, visits to the serene Abbey Falls, and a homestay experience amid aromatic spices.

Climax your trip in the Queen of Hills, Ooty, where toy train rides through the Nilgiri mountains, boating on the serene Ooty Lake, and hikes to Doddabetta Peak await. This all-inclusive package covers comfortable AC transfers, cozy stays (1N Mysore, 2N Coorg, 2N Ooty), daily breakfast, guided sightseeing, and entry fees.

Trip Plan
Day 01: Bangalore to Mysore

Meet & greet and drive towards Mysore.
Arrive Mysore & check in to the hotel.
Visit Maharaja Palace.
Art Gallery in the Jaganmohan Palace house.
Chamundi Hill – temple of Chamundeshwari, monoliths of Nandi and the gigantic Mahishasura statue.
Day at Leisure.
Overnight stay at the hotel.
 

Day 02: Mysore to Coorg

Visit Jayachamaraja Wodeyar Golf Club.
Back to hotel.
Breakfast at hotel & check out from the hotel.
Enroute visit Byalakuppe – largest Tibetan settlement in South India.
Arrive Coorg, Check in to the hotel.
Visit Omkareshwara temple.
Visit Raja Seat.
Return to hotel & overnight stay at the hotel.
 

Day 03: Coorg

Breakfast at hotel.
Visit Dubare Elephant Camp.
Visit Golden Temple – Tibetan monastery.
Visit Nisaragadhama.
Visit Abbey Falls.
Overnight stay at the hotel.
 

Day 04: Coorg to Ooty

Breakfast at hotel.
Check out from the hotel.
Arrive Ooty, & check in to the hotel.
Visit Botanical Garden.
Visit Market Place.
Visit Ooty Lake.
Day at Leisure.
Overnight stay at the hotel.
 

Day 05: Excursion to Coonoor

Breakfast at hotel.
Visit Sim’s Park in Coonoor.
Visit Lamb’s Rock.
Visit Dolphin’s Nose.
Return back to hotel.
Overnight stay at the hotel.
 

Day 06: Ooty to Bangalore

Breakfast at hotel.
Check out from the hotel.
Back to Bangalore, Drop to Airport / Railway Station / Bus Stand.
