Destination: Shravanabelagola, Hassan, Belur, Halebeedu, Royal City of Mysore

 

Itinerary in details:

 

Day 01: Bangalore / Hassan-Shravanabelagola, Hassan, Belur & Halebeedu. (180kms/4hrs) ( Meal – Lunch & Dinner)

Morning board your vehicle and drive to Hassan, en route visit Shravanabelagola -The monolithic statue of Lord Gomateshwara, a Jain saint and an object of worship for centuries, standing atop one of the hills. On arrival in Hassan, check in to your hotel. Lunch and proceed to visit i Belur & Halebeed Temple. Return to Hassan. Dinner and over night.

 

Day 02: Hassan / Mysore (120 Kms / 2 Hrs)(Meal- Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner)

Morning breakfast, check out at 08.00hrs, drive to Mysore, on arrival check in to your hotel. Lunch and half a day city tour – proceed to visit Chamundi Hill, Maharaja Palace, Art Gallery, and evening Palace lighting. Return to hotel. Dinner and overnight.

 

Day 03: Hassan / Bangalore (180kms/4 Hrs) :

Morning breakfast at 08.00hrs, checkout and drive to Bangalore en- route visit St.
Philomenas Church, Srirangapatna – Island fortress of Tippu Sultan. Visit Tippu’s
summer Palace (Dariya Daulat Bagh), Gumbaz, and Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple.

n arrival drop off at Taj West and to airport. Tour Ends.
