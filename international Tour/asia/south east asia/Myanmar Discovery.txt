Itinerary
Discover the Myanmar Top 5 Attractions and capture the spirit of Myanmar on this 11 Day journey . Along the journey, you’ll explore several of Myanmar’s greatest treasures, behold spectacular scenery, learn about different cultures, and experience the warmth and hospitality bestowed by the locals.

Trip Itinerary
 

DAY (1)          Arrival Yangon

Mingalar bar ! you will be welcomed by our Guide at Airport and transferred to hotel.

Start your Sightseeing of Yangon, formerly known as Rangoon. City tour, with as highlights our visit to the vibrant downtown area with its colonial-era buildings; the massive reclining Buddha at Kyaukhtatgyi Pagoda; Kandawgyi Park; the royal floating barge named Karaweik Palace; and the world-famous golden stupa, Shwedagon Pagoda, whose golden dome rises 98 meters above its base and is covered with 60 tons of pure gold.

Stay overnight in Yangon.

Included Activities : Arrival ; Touring in Yangon
Journey time : Yangon Airport to Yangon city centre ~ 1hr (Car)
Meals : Dinner
 

DAY (2)           Yangon- Golden Rock

Today we will drive out of Yangon to visit Kyaiktiyo Pagoda.  On the way, we will stop at Bago,an ancient capital of Mon Kingdom, which is famous for its traditional cheroot industry.

Proceed to Kyaikhtiyo(Golden Rock), a well-known Buddhist pilgrimage site in Myanmar. It is a small pagoda (7.3 metres (24 ft)) built on the top of a granite boulder covered with gold leaves pasted on by devotees. The balancing rock seems to defy gravity, as it perpetually appears to be on the verge of rolling down the hill.

Arrive at Kinmon Base Camp in the afternoon and transfer to a local truck to drive up to the terminal near the Golden Rock Pagoda (1–hour drive). Evening, visit the amazing Kyaiktiyo (Golden Rock) Pagoda and watch the spectacular sunset from the edge.Afterwards, explore the local shops in the area.

Overnight Stay in Kyaiktiyo.

Included Activities : Mt Kyaikhtiyo, Golden Rock Pagoda
Journey time : Yangon to Kinmon Base Camp ~ 4 hrs(Car)
Meals : Breakfast, Lunch
DAY (3)           Kyaiktiyo – Bago -Yangon

Enjoy the sunrise from Golden Rock, top of the mountain. Midmorning we will return Yangon via Bago through rice fields and small towns. At Bago, we visit to the massive 55-metre long Shwethalyaung Reclining Buddha and the four seated Buddhas. Complete the day by a visit to the local market and drive back to Yangon.

Stay overnight in Yangon.

Included Activities : Bago Tour
Journey time :  Kinmon Base Camp to Yangon ~ 4hrs (Car)
Meals :  Breakfast, Lunch
DAY (4)           Yangon – Inle Lake

After breakfast, transfer to the airport and short flight to Heho. Drive to Nyaung Shwe, the gateway of Inle Lake. Full-day tour of beautiful Inle Lake which is referred as the Venice of Asia, featuring stilt houses and villages, floating gardens, fishermen using ancient techniques such as leg-rowing, and, on the shores, workshops of traditional artisans, e.g. weavers and cigar-makers. Also included are visits to sanctuaries such as Phaung Daw Oo Pagoda and Nga Phe Chaung Monastery.

Stay overnight on Inle Lake.

Included Activities : Boat trip on the Inle Lake
Journey time : Excursion on Inle Lake ~ 6 hr (Boat)
Meals : Breakfast, Lunch
 

DAY (5)           Indein

After breakfast, visit to the colorful local market around Inle lake, where villagers from the surrounding area sell their products. Continue your exploration to the western banks of the lake and travel down a small canal to Indein – a Pa-Oh village which is known for its stupa-covered hill with 1,054 stupas constructed between the 17th and the 18th centuries. Spend time exploring Indein on foot, strolling around the village, visiting a local school and making your way to the top of the hill where you can wander amid the weather-beaten stupas and enjoy magnificent views of the lake below.

Stay overnight on Inle Lake.

Included Activities : Boat trip on the Inle Lake, explore Indein Ruins
Journey time : Excursion on Inle Lake ~ 7 hr (Boat)
Meals :  Breakfast, Lunch
 

DAY (6)           Inle – Mandalay

Today leave Inle lake for the flight to Mandalay, the last royal city of Myanmar Kingdom. Upon arrival, we will start the sightseeing toSagaing which has over 600 Buddhist monasteries where monks and nuns study and meditate. Visit pagodas such as Swan Oo Pon Nya Shin and U Min Thone Sae. Stop at the local market, Silverware cottage industry, clay pot making and Nunnery to learn the daily life of Buddhist nuns.

Next on this day tour is Amarapura,known as the City of Immortality. Visit Mahagandaryone Monastery which is the best place to experience the daily lives of the monks. After that, we will walk over the U Bein, the longest teakwood Bridgein the world.

Complete the day with the  visitMahamuni Buddha Image – a bronze Buddha image castled during the life time of Buddha himself.

Overnight stay in Mandalay.

Included Activities : Flyto Mandalay; Amarapura & Sagaing tour
Journey time : Heho to Mandalay ~ 45 Mins (Flight)
Meals :  Breakfast, Lunch
 

DAY (7)           Mandalay & Mingun

After breakfast, transfer to the jetty for a pleasant boat ride upriver to Mingun.Upon arrival, explore the ruins of the vast Mingun Pagoda. Continue to the beautiful Hsinbyume Paya and the Mingun Bell, weighting 87 tons and being the world’s largest intact bronze bell. Sail back to Mandalay.

Continue your exploration to the Mandalay Palace, Shwenandaw Monastery – noted for its exquisite woodcarvings, gold leaf beating workshop and Kuthodaw Pagoda – known for containing the world’s largest book.

Conclude the tour by visiting Mandalay Hill to watch the sunset and the panoramic view of the city. Overnight in Mandalay.

Included Activities : Boat trip to Mingun; Sunset at Mandalay Hill
Journey time : Mandaly to Mingun ~ 1hr (Boat)
Meals :  Breakfast, Lunch
 

DAY (8)          Mandalay – Mt Popa – Bagan

This morning leave Mandalay and 4 hours drive to Bagan. On the way we will visit Mount Popa, an extinct Vocalno with the spectacular views and the birth place of Myanmar Nats (sprits).At the foot of mountain, you could observe Nat Shrine contains a display of mannequin like figures representing the 37 Nat and is a major pilgrimage destination.

Stop at a local palm tree plantation and a typical Local Village. This relaxing break will give you the chance to observe the local working life. In the late afternoon we will arrive Bagan and transfer to hotel.

Stay overnight in Bagan.

Included Activities : Mt Popa day trip; Villages Tour
Journey time : Mandalay to Bagan ~ 4hr (Car)
Meals : Breakfast, Lunch
 

DAY (9)           Bagan

Today is the comprehensive tour of the beautiful ancient city of Bagan, one of Myanmar’s premier historical and cultural centres, home to more than 2,000 temples and pagodas ! Our tour includes shrines such as Ananda, Htilominlo, Thatbyinnyu, Dhammayangyi, Sulamani, and Shwezigon Pagoda, plus secular sights such as Nyaung U market and traditional workshops.

Late afternoon boat tour on the Irrawaddy River, finishing as the sun sets on riverside villages and temples.

Stay overnight at Bagan.

Included Activities : Bagan temple tour ; sunset at temple
Optional Activities : Temple bike tour
Meals : Breakfast, Lunch
 

 

DAY (10)        Bagan– Yangon

This morning we will explore the remaining highlight of Bagan by horse cart through the less visited temples. You can stop to explore some hidden gems of Bagan.

In the afternoon transfer to the airport for your onward flight to Yangon.

Evening in Yangon is at leisure with a car and guide available for further exploration or last minute shopping.

Stay overnight in Yangon.

Included Activities : Bagan temple tour, Fly to Yangon
Journey time :  Bagan to Yangon ~ 1hr (Flight)
Meals :  Breakfast, Lunch
 

 

DAY (11)         Departure

This morning is free for you to explore the city at your leisure. Transfer to Yangon Airport for your onward flight . Goodbye Myanmar and we hope to serve you next time.

Included Activities : Departure
Meals :  Breakfast
